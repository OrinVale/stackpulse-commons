# Governance and transparency

## Current stewardship

StackPulse Commons is currently maintained by one project steward. It is not
yet a registered charity, foundation, or company. The public project identity
is intentionally separate from the maintainer's personal information; this does
not change the project's obligation to keep accurate records or comply with
applicable law.

Before the project accepts large, recurring, or restricted funding, the
steward will obtain appropriate professional advice on the legal and accounting
structure.

## Commitments to supporters

- Publish code, methodology, and source links before publicly promoting a
  fundraising campaign.
- Keep contributions separate from personal wallets through a dedicated project
  treasury address.
- Publish a milestone update at least monthly while funded.
- Report funding received and spending by category, with dates and transaction
  references where safe to disclose.
- Protect supporter privacy: do not publish donor names, contact details, or
  personal invoices without consent.
- Disclose material delays, scope changes, source failures, and unspent funds.

## Spending policy

Project funds may be used for documented delivery and necessary operations:

- engineering and research time;
- source monitoring, hosting, domain, and transaction costs;
- reasonable workspace and connectivity needed to operate the project;
- independent review, compliance, and security work.

The operating-cost line is capped in the published pilot budget. Personal
spending unrelated to the project is outside this policy.

## Decision log

Material changes to methodology, scope, funding allocation, or source policy
will be recorded in the repository changelog or a public update before they are
represented as completed work.

## Conflicts and correction requests

Anyone may request a correction, removal, or source-policy review after the
public repository launches. Requests will be assessed against the methodology;
the project will not publish private information or remove accurate public
source links solely because they are inconvenient.
