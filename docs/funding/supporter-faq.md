# Supporter FAQ

## What does a contribution fund?

Contributions fund the milestone-based StackPulse Commons pilot: reliable
public-source monitoring, a documented Web3 research pilot, a no-login
dashboard, and transparent reports. The full budget is in
[FUNDING.md](../../FUNDING.md).

## Is this an investment or a token sale?

No. A contribution does not purchase equity, a token, a product entitlement,
or a financial return.

## Will I receive a tax receipt?

No. StackPulse Commons is not currently a registered charity or fiscal host
and cannot issue tax receipts.

## Why is there a public wallet address?

The project uses a dedicated EVM treasury so that supporters can independently
verify transfers. It does not make donor identity public: the project will not
publish donor names or contact details without consent.

## Which assets can I send?

The preferred route is USDC or ETH on Base. Check the asset and network before
sending. Do not send a test asset or use a network solely because the address
looks similar; different networks are distinct.

## Can StackPulse return a mistaken transfer?

No return can be guaranteed. Blockchain transfers are generally irreversible,
and contributors should verify the address and network before sending.

## How will progress be reported?

While funded, StackPulse will publish at least one delivery and funding update
per month, including completed work, material delays, next milestone, and
aggregate spending by category.
