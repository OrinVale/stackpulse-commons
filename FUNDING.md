# Funding policy and treasury

## Project status

StackPulse Commons is an independent open-source project. Contributions fund
the public deliverables and reporting described below; they do not confer
equity, tokens, services, preferential access, or financial return.

The public funding objective is **USD 12,000 over 90 days**. It is structured
as milestones, not a blank cheque.

| Engineering and maintenance | 4,800 | Source connectors, compact state, tests, dashboard foundation |
| Research and data quality | 2,400 | Source registry, taxonomy, sampling, methodology review |

### Milestone release plan

| Funding reached | What ships |
| --- | --- |
| USD 3,000 | Sustained source pipeline, source review, methodology refinement, and the first public changelog |
| USD 8,000 | Scoped Web3 source registry, taxonomy, quality-review sample, and initial open dataset |
| USD 12,000 | No-login dashboard, recurring report, and monthly delivery/funding update |

If less funding is received, the scope will narrow to the highest completed
milestone. The project will not represent a planned deliverable as completed.

## Transparency commitments

- Publish a delivery and funding update at least monthly while funded.
- Report contributions and spending by category, date, and transaction
  reference where safe to disclose.
- Retain records for project expenses and on-chain transactions.
- Protect donor privacy: do not publish donor names, contact details, or
  personal invoices without consent.
- Keep the project treasury separate from unrelated personal funds.
- Do not ask supporters to recycle donations or promise a financial reward.
